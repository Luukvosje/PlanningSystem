# 0005 — Marketing channel: Meta Ads as the first direction

- **Date:** 2026-08-16
- **Status:** final as a first direction, not verified against live market data
- **Touches:** [marketing.md](../../marketing.md)

## Decision

Start with narrowly targeted Meta Ads (interest/behaviour plus a geo radius), small test
budgets of roughly €10–20 a day, and video showing "Word → chaos → solution".

## Why

The audience — gyms, later small businesses generally — is narrow and local; broad advertising
is waste. A product you have to *see* working performs better in video than in static text.

## Alternative considered

Lookalike audiences. Only worthwhile after one or two paying customers; there is no basis for
them yet.

## Warning

No current CPM or targeting was checked. Verify the assumptions before putting budget behind
this.
