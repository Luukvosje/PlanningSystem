# 0001 — Product scope and non-goals

- **Date:** 2026-08-16
- **Status:** final — not to be changed without an explicit conversation
- **Touches:** everything

## Decision

The MVP does no payroll, no contract management, no leave administration, no expenses, no
invoicing, no HR files, no AI planning, no time tracking, no clock in/out and no certificates.

Every feature is tested against one question: **does this contribute to planning faster, fewer
mistakes, better overview, less WhatsApp?** If not, it does not belong in the MVP.

## Why

Focus on the core promise — building and sharing a week's planning in five minutes — for the
first customer (a chain of gyms). Scope creep is the biggest risk for an MVP built alongside a
job.

## Alternative considered

None. This is a boundary, not a choice between options.

## Deliberate future direction, not a promise

Growth into a broader business SaaS through the existing `Modules` system, plus a separate
employee app. Nothing in code or marketing anticipates that until it is explicitly called for.
